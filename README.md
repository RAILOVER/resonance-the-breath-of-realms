# Resonance - The Breath of Realms

Resonance is an interactive and contemplative game where the player must solve puzzles to progress. Voice usage is essential, so make sure to have a microphone ready! I used Gradium to capture voice audio and interpret both the vocal pitch and the spoken words.

For the game music, using headphones is strongly recommended. I used Space DJ in Google AI Studio to dynamically adapt the soundtrack based on your current location in the game and the tasks you have accomplished (try stepping away from the puzzles and you will hear, haha!).

The game features 3 distinct levels:
- **The Tutorial**: where everything is clearly guided and explained.
- **The Desert Realm**: where specific vocal actions must be performed in front of each stele, with an adjacent riddle acting as a hint.
- **The Ice Realm**: the most challenging part to build, as I wanted to integrate Gradium vocal interpretation. I intentionally displayed the live transcript so you can see which words are recognized. Say **"take it"** next to the ice block, and **"circle"** to transmute the relic into a circle and finish the game by walking into the white booth.

Enjoy! :)
